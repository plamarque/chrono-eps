Chrono EPS is a stopwatch app for PE teachers. Time a whole class, record lap taps, identify runners and keep results without paper.

Features:
• Multi-runner stopwatch with per-runner stop
• Lap recording with a tap
• Relay mode with current / next runner
• Named participants
• Saved race history
• Visual replay on a virtual track
• Touch UI for tablet and phone
• Local storage, works offline
